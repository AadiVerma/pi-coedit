Initial release of **pi-helper-desk**: the agent's plan as a purple progress bar above the
prompt, with a `/helpers` desk showing the plan checklist, a π pet card per subagent (8
hues, hop/blink animation), colored Running/Finished/Stopped group headings, and a
kind-colored live tool-call feed. Ported from the Helper Desk mod for Claude Code.
